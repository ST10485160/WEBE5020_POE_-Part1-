Admissions

Applications are open for learners who wish to join Marconi Beam Secondary School. We welcome students who are committed to academic excellence and personal growth.

Required Documents

  • Certified Copy of Birth Certificate
  • Latest School Report
  • Proof of Residence
  • Parent or Guardian ID Copy
  • Passport Photo

Application Process

  1. Collect an application form.
  2. Submit all required documents.
  3. Attend an admission interview if required.
  4. Receive confirmation of acceptance.
